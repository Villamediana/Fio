Privacy policy

Updated November 12, 2026 (Novelo's server and subscription)

In short: what you keep in Novelo stays on your phone. The app has no account, no ads and no tracking. Your voice becomes text on the phone itself. Only the text (and what can be seen in a photo) goes to an artificial intelligence service on the internet, to be organized into tasks, notes and a diary.

Who looks after your data

Novelo is made by Jose Miguel Villamediana Osorio, an independent developer in Brazil, who is responsible for the processing of data described here under Brazil's General Data Protection Law (LGPD). Contact: miguel.villamediana@outlook.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you keep (tasks, notes, diary, photos and the recordings the app keeps) stays only in the app's storage on your phone. Novelo has no server that stores this content and asks for no account or login.

Your voice

Recordings are turned into text on the phone itself, by an artificial intelligence model that runs on it. The audio never leaves the device. Once it is text, the audio is deleted, unless the sound itself is what is worth keeping (a melody you sing, for example); then it stays with that item, on the phone.

Your photos

The text in a photo is read on the phone (with Google's ML Kit, which runs on the device; it may send Google technical usage and performance data, never the photo). The model on the phone also describes what can be seen in it. When you pick photos from your gallery, the app only receives the ones you picked. Photos taken with the app's camera are also saved to your phone's gallery (Novelo folder).

What goes to the internet, and to whom

To organize what you said or wrote and to answer your questions, Novelo sends to an artificial intelligence service over the internet:

  • the text of what you said or wrote;
  • for a photo, the text read in it and the description made on the phone (or the photo itself, if you use your own intelligence);
  • what the model needs to understand the request: your open tasks, today's diary, the latest items kept and those that may be related.

Where it goes depends on what you choose in Settings:

  • Novelo's intelligence (the default): the request goes to Novelo's server, which sends it through OpenRouter to your plan's model. In the free trial these are free models from other companies, which may log and keep what they receive (some use it to improve their own models), under each one's policy. On Novelo+, only providers that neither keep nor train on what they receive. If you would rather your texts not go to them, use your own intelligence.
  • Your own intelligence: the request goes straight to the service you set up (OpenAI, Anthropic, OpenRouter, Azure or another), on your account and under its policy. Your key stays on the phone, encrypted by Android.

Novelo's server keeps neither these requests nor the answers: it only passes them on and counts.

Novelo's server

To know your plan and how much you used, the server keeps: a code for your phone, encrypted (made from the Android identifier, only so the free trial is one per phone), your plan and until when it lasts, how many talks and photos you organized each day and, if you subscribe, the Google Play subscription receipt. It also keeps overall counts of usage and model costs, with nothing that identifies you. Your internet address is only used encrypted, to prevent abuse. None of this is sold or shared.

Subscription

Novelo+ is charged by Google Play, which handles the payment: Novelo never sees your card details. Cancel anytime in the Play Store. Using your own intelligence is free and doesn't go through Novelo's server.

Downloads

The model that hears and sees on the phone is downloaded from Hugging Face, which, as with any download, sees the device's internet address.

Backup

The backup is a file the app writes to the folder you choose (on the phone or in a cloud folder you chose yourself). Novelo does not send the backup anywhere else.

Permissions

  • Microphone: to record what you say, only when you press the button.
  • Camera: to take photos inside the app.
  • Notifications and alarms: reminders, and the notice that the app is listening or organizing.
  • Internet: to organize with the intelligence and download the on-phone model.

What Novelo does not do

It shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking tools, and does not sell or share your data with anyone beyond what is described here. Google Play may collect install and crash data for the app, under Google's privacy policy.

How long, and how to delete

Your data stays on the phone until you delete it in the app or uninstall it. Uninstalling deletes everything except backup files you saved outside the app. You can see, correct and delete each item in the app itself. To delete what Novelo's server keeps about your phone, write to the email below. To exercise any of your rights or ask a question, write to miguel.villamediana@outlook.com.

Children

Novelo is not made for children under 13.

Changes

When this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it and the news shows up in the app's release notes.
